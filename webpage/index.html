Is Australia a Big Spender?

Governments around the world spend a substantial share of what their economies produce. But where does Australia sit, what are Australians paying for and how has its spending changed?

Are we actally spending that much

Government spending is measured in billions of Australian dollars. Compare revenue with expenses over the past decade to see how the relationship between the two has changed.

The gap is getting tighter.

In 2024-25, general government expenses reached $1,030.5 billion, exceeding revenue of $1,022.4 billion. The next question is whether this spending is unusually high relative to Australia's economy.

Source: Australian Bureau of Statistics, Government Finance Statistics, Annual 2024-25. Values are in current-price A$ billions. Expenses are displayed as positive amounts.
